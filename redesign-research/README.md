## Earlier plan in brief (superseded)

The current site looks strong and has real production work, but its four service groups and WhatsApp-led contact flow no longer describe the full Zavino offer. The approved homepage keeps **“WHERE VISION TAKES FLIGHT”** as a small all-caps eyebrow above **“Build it. Market it. Automate it.”**, followed by **“Branding, Marketing, Website development, content and AI automation.”** The design uses concept 03's type-first evergreen composition and image apertures that change quickly with scroll through short fades. The **five service pillars** remain clear in the next index. Real films and stills can prove production craft now. Web, SaaS, SEO, and AI pages should use clear scope, process, deliverables, and explicitly labeled Zavino-made demonstrations until approved client work is ready.

The visitor’s next step should be easy to choose: **send a project brief**, **book a 30-minute audit** at the supplied [Cal.com link](https://cal.com/zavino/audit), or **message Zavino on WhatsApp**. Each service page should carry its own context into the shared brief form. The exact audit promise, form destination, budget bands, and response time require business confirmation before launch.

## Working design principles

1. **Lead with the approved words.** The small all-caps eyebrow, exact headline, and supporting line are fixed in the [UI design base](ui-design-base.md). The five-pillar index gives visitors the formal service structure immediately after the hero.
2. **Show only work Zavino can substantiate.** Existing production assets are genuine portfolio material. In-house digital demos are visibly labeled. Client outcomes and logos require approval.
3. **Give each pillar its own composition.** Cinematic film for Content Production, campaign map for Digital Marketing, artifact system for Branding & Creative, working UI for Web Development, and workflow canvas for AI Automation.
4. **Make the hero react to scroll.** A small number of image apertures cycle quickly through approved stills with short fades as the visitor scrolls. Type and contact actions stay stable; reduced-motion users get the same offer with static images.
5. **Keep the lead path short.** An intent-specific page leads to proof and scope, then to the brief form, audit booking, or WhatsApp. CTAs name what opens.
