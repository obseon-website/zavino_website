# Handoff to the AI website builder

Use this as the short assignment for the future **design and implementation** pass. This file is a brief, not an instruction to edit code during the current research pass.

## Mission

Redesign Zavino into a **professional, high-converting AI automation agency website** that also sells SaaS development and comprehensive web development. Aim the positioning at substantial, connected business problems rather than small one-off tasks. Make the site credible to a buyer responsible for customer data, operational workflows, and product delivery. The ambition is to be among the best AI automation agencies; express that through clarity, craft, and technical depth, not an unsupported ranking claim.

The working homepage message is:

- Positioning label: **AI AUTOMATION AGENCY · SAAS DEVELOPMENT**
- Headline: **AI automation for the work your business runs on.**
- Support: **We connect customer and operational data, automate decisions and handoffs, and build the SaaS products that make it all work.**
- Primary action: **Discuss your project**

Read [Current direction](current-direction.md) for rationale and the full offer hierarchy. AI Automation and SaaS Development should dominate the front page; Web Development is the third primary offer. AI UGC creative, custom AI, content, marketing, branding, SEO, and offline work should remain discoverable later in the journey and through appropriate cross-links.

## Show your best frontend design work

Treat this as a chance to demonstrate exceptional art direction and implementation ability. Create a design that feels **authored for Zavino**: bold readable type, distinctive desktop and mobile compositions, precise rhythm, purposeful negative space, and a memorable luminous systems language built from evergreen/charcoal, warm ivory, and one electric accent. Do not default to the common AI-agency template of a purple gradient, glowing orb, three identical cards, fake dashboard, or endless reveal animation.

Design the static experience first, then make it feel alive. Build **multiple bespoke CSS animated elements with a controlled glow**: a signal path, active node halos, a capability edge glint, an approval-gate state, and responsive CTA/focus light. Add a few smaller, delightful interactions where they prove skill or clarify the offer. The [motion specialist brief](motion-and-interaction-direction.md) supplies scenarios, state behavior, timings, touch/keyboard handling, reduced-motion behavior, and performance limits. Improve the composition and implementation beyond the literal examples if you can do so without reducing clarity or accessibility.

The signature visual is an **illustrative customer-data-to-action workflow**: customer signal → connected data → AI/rules decision → human approval → offer or task → measurement. Make the approval point and failure/hold path visible. Offer two or three selectable scenarios. Label it **Illustrative workflow · Zavino concept** unless real client proof is available. A static view must communicate the same story.

## Build the whole buyer journey

Follow the [page map](service-architecture-and-page-map.md), [page blueprints](page-experience-blueprints.md), [conversion plan](conversion-funnel-and-lead-form.md), and [roadmap](redesign-roadmap.md). Each primary service page should explain the buyer problem, scope, deliverables, integrations or product boundaries, process, honest evidence, and a contextual brief/booking path. Let a visitor understand what Zavino can build and what happens after an inquiry.

Keep the full [NeuroXAI research](neuroxai-funnel-research.md) in mind. Its useful lesson is a specific **problem → offer → deliverables → process → proof → brief/booking** path, with industry constraints and candid labels for in-house work. Preserve that information in the research; use it to sharpen Zavino's own pages. Do not copy NeuroX's prices, guarantees, client counts, claims, page count, or wording.

Existing Zavino creative media can demonstrate production quality when rights and roles are verified. It cannot be presented as evidence of enterprise automation or SaaS delivery. Label concept systems and product demos plainly. No fabricated client outcomes, telemetry, testimonials, or MNC logos.

## Quality bar

- The first screen says **AI automation** and a meaningful business value, shows SaaS as a core service, and offers a clear next action.
- The visual design is outstanding on desktop and phone, with custom motion that looks made for this agency.
- All service text and actions work with touch, keyboard, static rendering, and reduced motion.
- The site stays fast enough to feel professional; animation must not delay content, block CTA use, or make forms sluggish.
- The form and direct booking paths are real and understandable. Never show a false submission success.
- Before writing code, follow AGENTS.md and read the relevant installed Next.js guides in node_modules/next/dist/docs/. The earlier typographic-reel hero files are an archive, not the current specification.

Bring strong independent design judgment. Make the final site more compelling than this brief's examples while keeping its business priorities, truthfulness, and buyer journey intact.
