# Current Zavino direction — AI automation, SaaS, and web

**Authoritative brief, updated 4 October 2026.** This replaces the old “Build it. Market it. Automate it.” hero, the creative-first five-pillar homepage, and the image-swapping hero plan. Preserve the earlier research as a record, and preserve the full [NeuroX AI study](neuroxai-funnel-research.md) as a source-linked reference. NeuroX's public claims and commercial terms are not Zavino's.

## The business position

Zavino is an **AI automation agency and SaaS development partner** for organizations with meaningful operational complexity. Lead with connected systems and substantial engagements, not small one-off tasks. The ambition is to become one of the best AI automation agencies; demonstrate that ambition through the site and delivery method rather than announcing an unsupported ranking.

**Best-fit buyer:** an owner of a consequential workflow in growth, operations, customer experience, product, or IT; a founder building a real SaaS product; or a team whose data, people, and software do not yet work together. Enterprise and multinational buyers should see competence in integration, access control, approvals, observability, failure handling, and handoff. Do not imply Zavino already serves named MNCs unless there is approved proof.

**Value proposition:** turn fragmented data and repeated handoffs into an AI-assisted system that takes the right action at the right time, with people in control and software built to last. The site should make this tangible through a customer-data-to-offer example, not vague “transform your business” language.

An alternative hero is acceptable only if it states **AI automation** and a concrete business benefit more clearly.

## What the animated example should explain

Use a clearly marked **“Illustrative workflow · Zavino concept”** example:

1. A customer action or lifecycle signal enters from a CRM, commerce, support, or product system.
2. The system joins consent, history, eligibility, and current business rules. Show the data sources and access boundaries rather than a magic AI box.
3. AI helps choose or draft a next action, such as a relevant offer or service response. A rules layer can reject an ineligible action.
4. A human approval point appears where the business needs it. The action waits rather than pretending to send automatically.
5. An approved email, WhatsApp message, in-app offer, or staff task is dispatched through the appropriate system.
6. Outcome and failure signals return for measurement and improvement.

The illustration should distinguish **example behavior** from a shipped product. Do not show invented customer data, real personal information, fabricated conversion lift, or a fake live dashboard. Frame “offer sending” as one possible use case; other serious workflows include support resolution, document operations, onboarding, revenue operations, and internal approvals.

## Offer hierarchy

| Level | What to show | Scope to explain |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Primary 1: AI automation** | First and most prominent service. | Workflow discovery, data and API integrations, AI agents and custom AI where appropriate, orchestration, guardrails, human review, deployment, monitoring, and iteration. Examples should be cross-system and measurable. |
| **Primary 2: SaaS development** | Equal strategic weight, clearly visible above the fold or immediately after the hero. | New SaaS products and modernization of prototypes: product discovery, UX, architecture, accounts and roles, data model, billing when needed, integrations, security design, QA, deployment, and ownership. |
| **Primary 3: Web development** | A strong third front door. | Corporate and campaign websites, ecommerce, portals, custom web apps, frontend/backend engineering, integrations, accessibility, performance, technical SEO, and continuous improvement. Explain fit rather than claim every possible stack or service is included by default. |
| **Adjacent capabilities** | Mention later on the homepage and more fully on Services or in discovery. | AI UGC creative, AI image/video production, custom AI experiences, content production, branding, paid marketing, SEO, and offline activations. They can support a wider engagement or be separately scoped, but should not dilute the front-page thesis. |

The sales journey can surface adjacent services **after acquisition or once the core problem is understood**: for example, a SaaS launch may need acquisition creative; a customer lifecycle system may need AI UGC variants and campaign operations. Tell the buyer about them at the relevant moment. The site may include a compact “More ways we help” area so those services are discoverable without competing in the hero.

## Homepage narrative and visual shift

1. **Hero: clear offer + signature system.** Keep the H1, support line, primary CTA, and a visible AI workflow scene in the first screen. The scene has a static useful state before it moves.
2. **The high-value problem.** Show disconnected data, manual handoffs, slow decisions, and inconsistent customer action as business problems. Avoid generic fear or impossible guarantees.
3. **Three core capabilities.** AI automation, SaaS development, web development. Give each a distinct composition, concrete outputs, and a route to depth.
4. **One deeper example.** Let a visitor inspect the customer-data-to-offer flow, including source, decision, approval, action, and feedback. Make the example visibly illustrative.
5. **Product engineering proof.** Show a labeled in-house SaaS or interface concept with real, working states. Explain roles, integration boundaries, and why the product is usable.
6. **How engagements run.** Discovery and workflow map → scoped design/prototype → build/integrate → launch/measure/iterate. State outputs and decision points.
7. **Credibility and adjacent work.** Use verified project material accurately. Existing creative films can demonstrate production taste, not enterprise automation outcomes. Introduce AI UGC and other capabilities here.
8. **Conversion.** End with a specific invitation to discuss a substantial workflow or product. Offer a short contextual brief and a direct scheduled conversation; keep WhatsApp and email as accessible shortcuts.

Visually, move from the earlier aviation and film-reel emphasis toward a **luminous operating-system language**: deep evergreen/charcoal, warm ivory, one controlled electric sage or cyan accent, precise diagram lines, high-quality typography, and generous space. Glows should reveal state and relationships rather than form generic AI wallpaper. Preserve recognizable Zavino brand character where it supports this shift. The [motion brief](motion-and-interaction-direction.md) defines the interactive treatment.

## Proof and copy rules

- Say what the team **can build** and how work would be evaluated. Do not convert a proposed workflow into a published case study.
- Label in-house work as **Zavino concept**, **Lab**, or **illustrative workflow**, with its actual state and limitations. Use approved client names, logos, numbers, and testimonials only with evidence and permission.
- Existing media proves creative production only unless the related software or automation role is documented.
- Give serious buyers specifics: data sources, integrations, who approves actions, what happens on failure, where IP and data live, how success is measured, and what handoff looks like. Do not promise certification or compliance outcomes without proof.
- Avoid small-task/package framing as the headline offer. Do not publish a minimum project price, turnaround guarantee, or enterprise client claim until Zavino can support it.
