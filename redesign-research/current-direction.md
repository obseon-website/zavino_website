# Current Zavino website direction

Updated 4 October 2026 from the user's latest brief. This direction supersedes the earlier five-pillar, creative-first homepage concept. The [NeuroX AI research](neuroxai-funnel-research.md) remains intact as a reference for specific offers, buyer journeys, honest proof, and contact paths. NeuroX's prices, claims, packages, and client results are not Zavino claims.

## Positioning

Lead with Zavino as an **AI automation agency** for substantial, connected business workflows. The homepage headline is “AI automation for the work that moves your business.” The value proposition is to turn scattered customer and operational data into useful decisions, timely actions, and working software. Address complex, cross-team work without claiming unverified enterprise client results or saying Zavino is already the “best.”

The three front-door capabilities are:

1. **AI automation:** customer lifecycle and offer orchestration, AI agents with human checkpoints, integrations, and measurement.
2. **SaaS development:** custom products, portals, dashboards, internal tools, and production-ready web applications.
3. **Web development:** business sites, ecommerce, web platforms, frontend/backend work, performance, technical SEO, and ongoing improvement.

Custom AI, AI UGC creative, branding, content, digital marketing, and offline work remain available as supporting or separately scoped services. They appear later in the journey so first-time visitors understand the core offer.

## Homepage structure

1. AI-first value proposition and a visibly labeled illustrative customer-data-to-offer system map.
2. Explanation of connected, business-wide problems.
3. Three core capability rows with concrete scope.
4. A second illustrative workflow that shows data → decision → approved action → learning.
5. Delivery approach: understand, build, launch and improve.
6. Additional capabilities and selected **creative** work, labeled so it does not imply AI/SaaS case studies.
7. Contact action via project conversation and direct audit booking.

This is a design and copy update. The current contact page offers booking, email, WhatsApp, and brief guidance; it does not yet implement the structured on-site lead form proposed in the earlier [funnel plan](conversion-funnel-and-lead-form.md). A form needs an agreed inbox or CRM destination and service/budget choices before implementation.

## What carries over from NeuroX research

- A clear offer linked to a concrete buyer problem, scope, process, and next step.
- Direct call booking alongside a way to send a brief.
- Honest labeling for in-house concepts and existing creative proof.
- Intent-specific service content before multiplying routes or claiming outcomes.
