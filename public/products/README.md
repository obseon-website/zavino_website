# Product assets

Collected on 13 October 2026 from the product previews supplied by Zavino.

- `outbid-logo.png` is the original Outbid.bd symbol from `/brand/logo.png` at [Outbid.bd](https://outbid-preview.obseon-host.workers.dev/).
- `outbid-poppins-600.woff2` is the Poppins 600 font used by that app's wordmark. Its use is isolated to the Outbid logo. `Poppins-LICENSE.txt` retains the SIL Open Font License from the [official Google Fonts repository](https://github.com/google/fonts/blob/main/ofl/poppins/OFL.txt).
- `forma-sample.png` is the original 1200 × 900 sample image offered by [Forma's workspace](https://forma-file-conv.thezavino.workers.dev/app). The Labs preview converts this local file with the browser canvas API; no uploads or remote conversion are involved.
- Forma's logo is composed of CSS shapes in the source app. `ProductLogo` reproduces those same three layers: 11 × 23 at (2, 5), 11 × 18 at (10, 2), and 11 × 11 at (18, 0), with 4px corners, a −10° group rotation, and original colors `#3448ed`, `#6b7afa`, and `#b8c0ff`. The mark and lettering retain the product's branding inside Zavino's existing visual system.

The adjacent JSON files record the original asset URLs and retrieval method. No generated substitute logos are used.
